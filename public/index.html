Who We Are

Fire, Smoke & Heart

Born and raised in Streetsboro, Ohio, Game Night Grillers started as a backyard crew that took competition BBQ way too seriously — and we have zero regrets. What began over dice and dominoes evolved into a championship-caliber pit team that shows up ready to compete every single time.

We believe great barbecue isn't luck. It's obsession. Every rub is dialed in, every fire is managed by hand, and every cut of meat earns its place on the plate. We bring the same intensity to the pit that we bring to game night.
